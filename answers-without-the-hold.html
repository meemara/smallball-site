Answers without the hold.

A tech in the field has a question. The old answer was: call the manufacturer, sit on hold, wait while someone searches the same documentation you could have read yourself.

The problem

Every trade runs on equipment from a handful of manufacturers, and every manufacturer has a help line. When a technician hits something they don't recognize — a quirk, an error, an integration detail — the reflex is to call. Then comes the hold music, the hand-off, and the wait while a help-desk rep searches their own manuals for the line the tech needs. The answer was usually already written down somewhere. The cost was never the answer. It was the time spent getting to it — multiplied by every tech, every call, every job.

What runs

Mine works like this:

  • We built our own searchable knowledge base from the manufacturers' published documentation — across the brands my technicians work with every day.
  • A tech asks a question in plain language and gets a direct, sourced answer in seconds — pulled from the actual manuals, integration guides, and known-issue notes.
  • It lives on a simple web app any tech can open from the field, on whatever device is in their hand. No login queue, no hold music, no hand-off.
  • When a tech learns something the documentation doesn't cover, that hard-won fix can be written back in — so the next person gets it for free.

The numbers

Instant answer instead of a help-line call Thousands of manufacturer documents, searchable at once Available to every tech, on any device, in the field

The honest part

This one isn't about saving me time — it's about giving my technicians their time back, and getting customers a fixed system sooner. The answer a tech needs almost always exists in the manufacturer's own documentation; the friction was finding it while standing in someone's mechanical room. Put the knowledge where the work happens and the hold music disappears. The judgment stays with the tech — the machine just does the reading and the searching, instantly.
