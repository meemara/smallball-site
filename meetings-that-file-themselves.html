Meetings that file themselves.

The most expensive part of a meeting is the hour after it — writing up what was decided and who owes what. So mostly nobody does, and the decisions evaporate.

The problem

I'm in around 15 meetings a week, across two companies — in person, on Teams, on Zoom. The notes problem isn't taking them — it's that the write-up, the action items, and the “wait, what did we decide?” lookup all depend on somebody spending at least 15 minutes after every single call doing homework. That homework never survives a busy week.

What runs

Now, automatically, for every meeting:

  • Whether it's in person (a recorder running), on Teams, or on Zoom, the conversation gets captured.
  • Each one becomes structured notes: summary, decisions made, action items with owners, filed to the right company's folder.
  • The durable stuff — who people are, what was agreed, the gotchas — flows into a knowledge base I can question later in plain English.

The numbers

~15 meetings a week, filed automatically At least 15 minutes of write-up saved per meeting In person, Teams, or Zoom

The honest part

I built this for myself before I ever offered anything like it to a client. It's also the system I use to run client engagements — the notes from our sessions file themselves the same way. I eat my own cooking.
