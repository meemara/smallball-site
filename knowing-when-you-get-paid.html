Knowing the minute you get paid.

“Did that invoice ever get paid?” sounds like a question about cash. In our business it's really a question about what happens next — and that's where the real cost was hiding.

The problem

When an invoice gets paid, things are supposed to move: the warehouse orders material for that project, the project team kicks off their next steps. But all of that depended on a person noticing the payment and passing the word. Miss the signal, and the warehouse orders late, the project team starts late, and a schedule slips by days — over something as small as nobody refreshing a screen.

What runs

One small watcher fixed it:

  • Every 10 minutes through the workday, it checks invoice status in our project system.
  • The moment an invoice flips to PAID, the nine people who depend on that signal get told automatically.
  • The warehouse orders product on time. The project team fires off their process. Nobody has to notice — the trigger pulls itself.

The numbers

9 people notified the instant it's paid Warehouse orders on time; project steps don't slip Checks every 10 minutes, 6 AM–8 PM

The honest part

This one isn't a big time-saver, and I won't pretend it is. The win is that a payment clearing now automatically triggers the next moves instead of waiting on someone to catch it. A missed trigger there used to cost days on a schedule. Now it can't be missed — that's worth far more than the few minutes of checking it also removed.
This runs in my own operation today.
